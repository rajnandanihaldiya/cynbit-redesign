Questions

Before you get in touch

Do I need clean data to start?

No. Part of discovery is reviewing where your data lives and what needs tidying first.

Will AI replace my team?

Our automations take over repetitive steps so your team can focus on work that needs judgement. People stay in control of decisions.

How long does a first automation take?

It depends on the process and systems involved. You’ll get a timeline in the plan after the discovery call.

Can it work with our existing tools?

Usually, yes. We connect to common CRMs, accounting tools, email and spreadsheets, and check integrations during discovery.

Have a process in mind?

Describe the task you’d like to automate. We’ll suggest an approach.
